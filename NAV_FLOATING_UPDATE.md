# Floating navigation update

- Converts the fixed full-width bar into a centered floating navigation shell.
- Adds rounded corners, glass blur, border, and responsive shadow depth.
- Shrinks slightly after scrolling.
- Keeps active-link indicators inside the rounded container.
- Adds softer rounded hover treatments to portal and primary CTA links.
- On mobile, the floating nav stays visible above the full-screen menu and switches to the inverse logo while open.
- Adds a responsive `--nav-h` token for pages that offset content below the navigation.
